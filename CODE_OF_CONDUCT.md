# Code of Conduct

## Standard

This is a technical project. Be direct, be accurate, and be decent about it.

**Expected:** focus on the technical substance, accept correction gracefully, cite sources, and assume good faith in disagreement. Saying "I don't know" and "I was wrong" are signs of competence here, not weakness.

**Not accepted:** harassment, personal attacks, demeaning newcomers for not knowing something, or sexualized or discriminatory language.

## Domain-specific expectations

This project explains encryption, including how ransomware uses it, so a few things go beyond
ordinary conduct:

- **Do not post malware samples or links to them.** This site is about concepts; samples have no
  place in it.
- **Do not post data from a real incident**, even redacted. It is rarely as anonymous as it looks.
- **Do not request operational or recovery tooling.** See the scope section of
  [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md). Framing it as research does not change the answer.
- **Correct wrong cryptography publicly and without drama.** People learn from this site, so a
  confidently wrong explanation that goes unchallenged does real damage.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues by opening an issue, or privately to the maintainers if the report itself is sensitive. Maintainers will respond and may edit, remove, or reject contributions, or ban contributors, for behaviour they judge inappropriate.
